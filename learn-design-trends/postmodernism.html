Philosophy

Postmodernism is a design philosophy that revels in the playfulness of breaking away from traditional design conventions.

It emerged in the latter half of the 20th century and often combines various design styles, creating eclectic and diverse spaces.

Characteristics

Postmodern design can incorporate elements of irony, humor, and cultural references into its creations.
It challenges the notion of a single, dominant design style and encourages the freedom to experiment and blend influences, resulting in visually intriguing and thought-provoking interiors and architecture
