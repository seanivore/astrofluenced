Philosophy

Sleek almost isn't enough of a descriptor for Mid-Century Modern perfectionism. It is as if the goal was to make a formless-form: a chair that appears as if it grew naturally that way.

It should be of no surprise that Mid-Century Modern design places an emphasis on functionality with a connection to nature. It often features large windows and open spaces.

Characteristics

This retro style is characterized by its soft curves, organic forms, and iconic vintage pieces. It emerged in the mid-20th century and remains influential today.
