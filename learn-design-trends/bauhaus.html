Made For The Unity

Conscious attempt to bring art and functionality together.

Philosophy

Bauhaus is a modernist design movement that emphasizes aesthetics that aid in practicality.

Emerging in the early 20th century, the core idea is that art and design need to be united in functionality.

Characteristics

It has had a profound influence on modern architecture and interior design.
Bauhaus design is characterized by geometric shapes, simplicity, and the "form follows function" principle.
