Philosophy

Art Deco is a glamorous design style that flourished in the 1920s and 1930s. The design was a bold solution for a bold era in history.

Characteristics

Art Deco exudes glamour and luxury. It is known for its opulent use of materials like glass and metals, as well as unflinching contrast of colors.
It is marked by its elegant, geometric motifs and symmetrical, geometric designs.
