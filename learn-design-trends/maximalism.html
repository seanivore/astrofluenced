Made For The Excess

The art of making excess not feel like clutter.

Philosophy

Maximalism is an expressive design style that embraces excess. It is a balancing game, matching the unmatchable, and making the unsymmetrical layout feel symmetric.

Characteristics

Maximalist interiors are characterized by a sense of opulence and abundance.
All about the excess, the design embraces vibrant colors, bold patterns, and a mix of styles to create visually rich and eclectic spaces.
