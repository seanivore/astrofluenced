Personal Growth from Introspection

Tap into transformative energies for growth: Grounding with Moon in Taurus, passion through Venus sextile Mars, and introspection via Mercury trine Pluto. Ignite personal power and embrace positive change.
When our ruler of thought and speech, Mercury, is in Virgo and opposing dreamy Neptune in equally dreamier home of Pisces, our ability for finding clarity is challenged. It can become difficult to discern the truth to navigate challenges in life. The fact that Neptune is retrograde in Pisces only amplifies this.
